自傳 

林雨晴 Ivy Lin，高雄人。第一份打工在巷口的麵包店，從那時起就喜歡看著客人帶著剛出爐的麵包離開。

從門市人員一路做到門市營運主任，排班、新人培訓、營收追蹤都是日常。遇到問題時，我會先到現場看一輪，再和同仁一起想辦法。

下班後常去其他品牌的店裡走走，觀察他們怎麼排陳列、怎麼招呼客人，回來再挑適合的做法試試看。

下一步想往多店營運的方向走，把現場經驗整理成能複製的做法，讓每家店都能穩穩運作。
Ivy Lin, from Kaohsiung. My first part-time job was at a small bakery around the corner, where I fell in love with fresh bread and happy regulars.

Starting on the shop floor, I grew into a store supervisor who plans rotas, coaches newcomers and watches daily takings closely.

On days off I wander through other shops, noting how they arrange shelves and greet guests, then try the best ideas back at our counter.

Next, I want to look after several shops at once and turn what works on the floor into steps any branch can repeat.
